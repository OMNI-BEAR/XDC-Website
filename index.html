Xavier Debate Circle

Stand and Deliver • Est. 1996

Founded in 1996, the Xavier Debate Circle is the official debate and public speaking organization of Xavier University. For three decades, we have cultivated intellectual excellence, fearless discourse, and transformative leadership among Atenean debaters and advocates.

Purpose & Vision

Mission

The Xavier Debate Circle is dedicated to advancing international debating standards among Xavier University students. By cultivating open-minded, critically sharp, and socially aware debaters, we foster a culture of rigorous argumentation, ethical discourse, and intellectual courage.

Vision

We envision ourselves as a premier bastion of intellectual excellence and fearless discourse—a transformative arena where Atenean minds are forged through competitive debate, community leadership, and the relentless pursuit of truth.
