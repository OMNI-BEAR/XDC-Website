XAVIER DEBATE CIRCLE

Stand and Deliver • EST. 1996

Founded in 1996, the Xavier Debate Circle is the official debate and public speaking organization of Xavier University – Ateneo de Cagayan—home to three decades of critical inquiry, championship advocacy, and Atenean discourse.

Purpose & Horizon

Mission

The Xavier Debate Circle is dedicated to advancing international debating standards among Xavier University students. By cultivating open-minded, critically sharp, and socially aware debaters, the organization fosters holistic personal development and empowers the youth sector to serve as a catalyst for community-wide civic engagement.

Vision

The Xavier Debate Circle envisions itself as a premier bastion of intellectual excellence and fearless discourse, a transformative arena where Atenean minds are forged through competitive fire, empowered with unshakable conviction, and united in elevating the standard of thought in society.
